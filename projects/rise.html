RISE: 2D Platformer

A Unity and C# 2D platformer with responsive movement, enemy AI, and dynamic lighting.

About the project

RISE is a 2D platformer built in Unity with C#. The goal is to reach the key at the end of the level. The player runs, jumps, double-jumps, and dashes with responsive controls while dealing with patrolling and guarding enemies, damaging traps, and out-of-bounds death lines. A UI shows health, coins, and health pickups. I also designed the levels and added fire-lamp lighting to tie the mechanics together. The audio manager uses scriptable audio templates that do not depend on MonoBehaviour, so many sounds can be handled with custom volume and feature controls. Shared systems are built around singletons.

Key features

  • Responsive run, jump, double-jump, and dash movement
  • Patrolling and guarding enemy AI
  • Damaging traps and out-of-bounds death lines
  • Fire-lamp dynamic 2D lighting
  • Level design built around reaching the key at the end
  • UI for health, coins, and health pickups
  • Health, restart, and menu systems
  • Audio manager with scriptable audio templates independent of MonoBehaviour
  • Singleton-based shared systems
