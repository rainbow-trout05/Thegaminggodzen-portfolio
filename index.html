About

I'm a cybersecurity student at Obafemi Awolowo University, graduating in 2029, and I'm teaching myself game development in Unity and C#. I started with Flappy Bird, then moved on to Pong and Asteroids through Harvard's CS50 and Animal Stampede through the Unity Junior Programmer Pathway. Today I keep learning through Udemy courses and by building games that are fun to play. My taste comes from indie games like Hollow Knight, where every jump and dash feels deliberate, and I want to make that responsiveness in my own work.

My most complete project so far is a 2D platformer where the goal is to reach the key at the end of the level. I programmed double jumps and dashes, patrolling and guarding enemy AI, damaging traps, out-of-bounds death lines, and a UI showing health, coins, and health pickups. I also did the level design and added fire-lamp lighting to tie the mechanics together. On the audio side, I built an audio manager with scriptable audio templates that don't depend on MonoBehaviour, so I can handle many sounds with custom volume and feature controls. I'm now building a 3D RPG where the player clicks anywhere to move and fight hordes of enemies. So far it has player movement, state machines, enemy AI, screen shake, tweening, and UI integration. I'm also using singletons and namespaces to keep a scalable architecture, for example a player controller that depends on separate movement and combat modules.

My security background shapes how I work. In a cybersecurity awareness hackathon (CTF), I took first place by identifying web application vulnerabilities, and that habit of asking how a system can fail carries over into debugging and testing my gameplay code. I'm looking for an internship, junior, or freelance gameplay programming role, remote, regional, or studio-based, where I can contribute to player movement, combat, enemy AI, and game feel while learning from an experienced team.
